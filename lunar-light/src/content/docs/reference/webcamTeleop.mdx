**How this code works**:
This code acts as an ad-on to the `HiveVisionOpmode.java` that was created at the end of `Webcam` installation. It simply tracks the desired entities in your Webcam's FOV and will drive towards it. This OpMode is likely not to use during a match but can be taken for inspiration on use cases for your team.
In order to leave manual mode, and force your robot to detect entities and move towards them, you must enable `Auto-Chase` as shown below.

During Teleop:<br></br>
• Right Bumper (RB): Toggles Auto-Chase mode ON or OFF<br></br>
<Aside type="tip">During Auto-Chase, your robot will spin and go to balls on its own, do not use your joystick during this state.</Aside><br></br>
• Left Stick (Y-axis): Drives Forward / Backward.<br></br>
• Left Stick (X-axis): Strafes Left / Right (Mecanum functionality).<br></br>
• Right Stick (X-axis): Turns Left / Right in place.<br></br>


<Aside type="note">This code will ONLY work on a Webcam, please look at the usages menu for your camera. </Aside>
