**How this code works**:

During the initializing period:<br></br>
• D-pad UP: Selects RED balls only. <br></br>
• D-pad DOWN: Selects BLUE balls only.<br></br>
• D-pad LEFT: Selects both RED and BLUE balls.<br></br>
• D-pad RIGHT: Selects all colors (RED, BLUE, and YELLOW).<br></br>

During Teleop:<br></br>
• Right Bumper (RB): Toggles Auto-Chase mode ON or OFF<br></br>
<Aside type="tip">During Auto-Chase, your robot will spin and go to balls on its own, do not use your joystick during this state.</Aside><br></br>
• Left Stick (Y-axis): Drives Forward / Backward.<br></br>
• Left Stick (X-axis): Strafes Left / Right (Mecanum functionality).<br></br>
• Right Stick (X-axis): Turns Left / Right in place.<br></br>
• Left Bumper (LB): Runs the Intake manually at full power (stops running when released).<br></br>


<Aside type="note">This code will ONLY work on a Limelight 3A, please look at the usages menu for your camera. </Aside>
