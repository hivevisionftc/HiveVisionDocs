**How this code works**:

Run `CVTest` to play the program, your robot will attempt to find an ideal path between the etities
`yellow_pollen` for example.

When `gamepad1.a` is pressed, the robot will spline through the pollen/nectar, once it sees balls in the FOV.

<Aside type="tip">During the period of TeleOp before `gamepad.a` is pressed, confirm that there is susbtance in camera view or else the robot will **not** be able to complete an effective spline</Aside><br></br>


<Aside type="note">This code will ONLY work on a Limelight 3A, please look at the usages menu for your camera. </Aside>
<Aside type="caution">This code will ONLY work if you have a tuned PedroPathing constants</Aside><br></br>

## Adapt to your robot:
<Steps>
    1. Measure how high your limelight is from the ground in inches, subtract `1.4` from that number, and update `CAMERA_HEIGHT` in `CVConstants` to this number
    2. Measure the angle that your limelight is pointing downwards in degrees, (Pointing straight means 0!), put that number inside the `CAMERA_PITCH` in `CVConstants` inside the `Math.toRadians()`
    3. Measure how far forward your limelight lens is from the center of the robot in inches, put this number inside `FORWARD_OFFSET` in `CVConstants`
    4. In `CVPathGen` update your limelight configuration name to the one listed in your driver station on line 39
    5. In `CVPathGen` update your pipeline to the one that contains Hive Vision in your limelight on line 41.

</Steps>

Congrats, you now have Teleop - Pathing program set up for your Limelight 3A. Simply follow the directions on how to use the program at the top. Thank you for using Hive Vision!
    <Aside type="note">Make sure that when your opmode is active, you click `gamepad1.a` to actually execute the paths!</Aside><br></br>
