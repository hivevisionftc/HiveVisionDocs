**Hive Vision** is a FTC computer vision package that can be integrated at your camera of choice. Currently Hive Vision Supports
integration with:
- <Badge text="Limelight 3A " variant="note" />
- <Badge text="Webcam" variant="caution" />

## Limelight 3A

Hive vision offers support for limelight 3A in the form of an all-inclusive neural networking package that can simply be added to your pipeline to get tracking started.
You can also refer to the `Usages` tab on how our community has built code around this package.a

## Webcam

Though not recommended, Hive vision offers a full package for HSV color detection for an installed tracking system on your Webcam. Limelight 3a generally is favored due to it having its own processor, and requiring cleaner operation due to its functionality not being impacted by the FTC SDK. However, our `Usages` tab also shows how our community members have been able to utilize this built in Hive Vision pipeline for Webcam
